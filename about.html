About Howsway

More than a brand, it's an experience — here's the story behind the HS. mark.

Who We Are

Howsway is a South African streetwear brand built on one idea: more than a brand, it's an experience. Every piece is designed and sampled in-house before it ever reaches a rack.

We keep our runs small on purpose, so what you're wearing stays considered rather than mass-produced. That same standard carries into everything else we build under the HowSway name, from the clothing to the events we host.

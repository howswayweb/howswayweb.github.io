Behind The Design: The Utility Jacket

HowSway Journal • Design Notes

The HS. Utility Jacket started as a single sample, cut to test a boxy silhouette we weren't sure would work outside a lookbook. A year on, it's one of the most requested pieces in the HowSway catalogue.

Starting with the shape

Every HowSway piece begins the same way — a shape we want to see more of, sketched, sampled, and worn by the team before it ever goes into a proper production run. The utility jacket came out of wanting an oversized layer that still held structure, with enough hardware detailing to make it feel deliberate rather than thrown on.

We went through three sample rounds before the collar and pocket placement felt right. That's slower than most streetwear labels are willing to go, but it's the standard we hold every drop to.

Small batches, on purpose

We don't run big numbers on any single piece. Keeping batches small means the fit stays consistent from the first jacket off the line to the last, and it means what you're wearing isn't on every second person in the room.

It also means when a drop sells out, it stays sold out — we'd rather do it again properly next season than rush a reprint.

More than a brand

That same care carries into everything else we build under the HowSway name. If you want to see it in a completely different setting, our Fine Dining Experience is where the clothing philosophy meets the table.
